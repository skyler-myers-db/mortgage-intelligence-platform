The Module 0 talk track and the synthetic canonical trio (B-48291 Rodriguez,
B-48294 Park, B-48295 Thompson) were re-anchored from Atlanta/GA to Chicago/IL
in Slice 8 of the real-data migration (April 2026).

**Why:** Atlanta/GA is not in the Cotality Delta Share footprint (the share
covers IL / CA / FL / TX / WA / CO per `docs/data-sources-gap-analysis.md §1`).
`docs/data-contract-module0.md §10` recommends Chicago as the demo-strongest
metro: 1.86M IL properties, 1.13M with open liens, highest average 1st-position
rate in the share at 4.75%, broadest cohort mix (both the 565K post-2023
refi pool and the 1.22M locked-in 2020–2022 cohort tell cleanly from IL).

**How to apply:** When writing talk-track copy, click paths, Genie sample
questions, or demo fixtures, use Chicago / Cook County / IL as the anchor and
60611 / 60647 / 60613 as the canonical ZIPs. Do NOT regress to Atlanta/GA/30309
unless explicitly asked. Geography drill-down is still a hero surface across
all 6 states — the anchor is Chicago, not a hard filter. Blocked segments
("Listed for Sale" needs MLS; "Permit Activity" needs Permits) should be
called out honestly in copy, not hidden or faked.
