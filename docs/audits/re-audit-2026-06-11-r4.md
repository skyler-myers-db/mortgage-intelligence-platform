## Buyer-Wow Annex — what would take the walkthrough over the top

Grounded in what's live today (every item builds on an existing surface; no new data dependencies unless noted). Effort: S = hours, M = a day-slice, L = multi-day.

1. **⌘K command palette (S–M).** The search is wired and fast — elevate it: `⌘K` opens a palette that searches borrowers/ZIPs/cities *and* actions ("approve queue for 60617", "open Cook County drill", "ask Genie…"), arrow-key navigable. Execs watch their team fly through the app keyboard-first. Highest wow-per-line-of-code in the product.
2. **KPI count-up + sparkline draw-in on Home load (S).** The first five seconds of every walkthrough is four static numbers. A 600 ms count-up with the sparkline drawing left-to-right (respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`) makes the same numbers feel alive. Pure CSS/JS, tokens already in place.
3. **"Tell the story" on Borrower 360 (M).** One button: Genie composes a 3-sentence plain-English narrative from the dossier + proof inputs ("This Chicago investor holds 41 properties; their 10.04% lien sits 356 bps above market with 93% equity — a refi+HELOC conversation pays for itself"). The pipeline already exists (Genie + evidence + guardrails); this is the moment a buyer realizes the AI *explains* leads, not just ranks them. Route the output through the existing numeric-claims verifier.
4. **Animated geography transitions + ZIP borrower constellation (M).** The drill chain is the hero flow but each level snaps statically. Animate the state→county zoom (300 ms ease), pulse the choropleth on data refresh, and at ZIP level render top borrowers as score-weighted dots that settle into the tile grid. The map is where buyers lean in.
5. **Funnel Sankey on Analytics Executive (M).** Marketable 5.16M → ITM → high-opportunity → offers → approved → actioned, as a flowing Sankey instead of the bar list. The numbers exist on the page already; this turns the value story into a single glance. (Pipeline Metrics keeps the precise figures below.)
6. **Morning briefing card on Home (M–L).** "Since yesterday: +N newly in-the-money, M crossed the 75 bps trigger, K new permits/listings (when feeds land)." The snapshot/delta scaffolding ("first snapshot · deltas pending") is literally waiting for this. This is the retention hook that turns a demo into a daily habit — and the strongest possible answer to "why is this better than my CRM report?"
7. **Campaign ROI projector in Portfolio Builder (S–M).** Next to projected conversion, an editable-assumptions strip: conversions × avg balance × margin ⇒ "$2.3M projected origination revenue from this build." CFO-bait; all inputs already exist in the build KPIs.
8. **Evidence-chip hover micro-preview (S).** Hovering any evidence chip shows a 2-row popover (source table, freshness, one value) before committing to the full drawer. Keeps the demo's pace while *increasing* how often evidence gets shown — the differentiator gets demoed more, not less.
9. **Booth kiosk mode (M).** `?kiosk=1` auto-cycles Home → drill → dossier → proof → Genie answer on a 90-second loop with caption overlays, as the attract loop between conversations at the FSI booth. Reuses the e2e walk path.
10. **Genie follow-up chips + "pin answer to Home" (M).** After each Genie answer, offer 2 context-aware follow-ups and a pin-to-Home action (the governed-actions pattern already supports this). Closes the loop from question → insight → standing artifact.

Sequencing for June 15: 2 + 8 (hours, zero risk) → 1 (palette, ship behind a flag) → 3 (the demo's emotional peak) → 4/5 if the week allows; 6/9/10 are the post-Summit product story.
