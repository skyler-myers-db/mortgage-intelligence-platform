## Headline takeaways for the main agent

1. **Finding #4 (timestamp tz drift)** is the subtle one — renders fine on a laptop in California but an hour off in every European customer's browser. Test by running the browser in `TZ=Europe/Paris` before the demo.
2. **Finding #10 (no AbortController)** is the silent resource leak — every filter-flip leaks a warehouse query + an audit row.
3. **Finding #12 (approvals in-memory only)** is the demo-bomb: approve a borrower, reload, borrower appears pending again. Presenter will trip on this live.
4. **Finding #18 (zero RBAC)** and **#19 (catalog hardcoding)** are the two "first customer deploy" BLOCKER/MAJOR items. Both are invisible in Entrada's own workspace.
5. **Finding #24 (500-row LIMIT)** is the "looks fine in synthetic, breaks at production scale" issue — should be paged or predicate-pushed.
